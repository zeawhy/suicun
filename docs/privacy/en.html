SuiVault (随存) Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19
中文版：隐私政策（中文）

SuiVault is a local-first privacy note-taking application.

Data Storage

All your records and photos are stored strictly locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. We have no user account system and do not collect or store any of your personal information.

Sync

If you configure WebDAV sync, data is transferred directly between your device and your own server, and we cannot access it. Sync configuration information is stored locally only.

Permissions

Camera is used only to capture ID document photos (saved to the app's private directory); photo gallery is used only to select ID document photos; fingerprint/face recognition is used only to unlock the app lock (biometric information is managed by the operating system); network is used only when you enable WebDAV sync.

Third Parties

There are no ads, no third-party SDKs, and no analytics tracking or telemetry in the app.

Data Deletion

You can completely delete all local data at any time under "Settings → Clear all data".
